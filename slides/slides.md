>>>

  + not on what we've **earned**
  + but on God's **grace** toward us
+ instead of boasting in my **capability**:
  + **accomplishments**, skills, talents
+ boast in my **inability**:
  + complete **reliance** on God's power:

## [Psalm 127](# "ref")

2. **Sleep** [(v2)](# "ref")

<!-- .element: class="r-fit-text" -->

  + **security** (safety, peace)

<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-K3uOmmlQmOo-pencil_shavings.jpg") %> -->
Unless the Lord **builds** the house, <br/>
the builders labor **in vain**.

[Psalm 127:1a (NIV)](# "ref")

>>>
+ "build": **projects** / endeavours:
  + education, **career**
  + "house": **family**, legacy
+ "in vain": cf. *hebel* (Eccl):
  + breath, fleeting, **empty** (Abel) 
+ but here (*shav*): worthless, **deceptive**
  + false: morally **negative**
+ not mere **prediction** of outcome
  + i.e., "you could **try**, but you'll fail"
+ but a moral **judgment**:
  + "you build according to a **lie**"
  + deceived by **value** system 
  + contrary to God's **truth**
+ not just "**worldly**" projects (money)
  + but even projects done **for God**:
+ e.g., King **David**, when secure in reign
  + proposed a great **project** for God:

---
<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-DtHTWqkgBZc-forest_house.jpg") %> -->
After **David** was settled in his **palace**, <br/>
he said to Nathan the prophet,

“Here I am, living in a **house of cedar**, <br/>
while the **ark** of the covenant of the Lord <br/>
is under a **tent**.”

[1 Chronicles 17:1 (NIV) (p.1/3)](# "ref")

>>>
+ big **investment**: labour, money
  + David figured he had **resources**
  + do something **great** for God
+ what was God's **response**?

---
<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-DtHTWqkgBZc-forest_house.jpg") %> -->
**You are not** the one <br/>
to build Me a house to dwell in.

[7](# "ref")
**I took you** from the pasture, [...]<br/>
and appointed you **ruler**
over My people Israel.

[1 Chronicles 17:4,7 (NIV) (p.2/3)](# "ref")

>>>
+ God does not **need** my projects
  + like a **child** "helping" in kitchen
+ It is **God** who has built **us**
  + holds our **futures**
+ God continues:

---
<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-DtHTWqkgBZc-forest_house.jpg") %> -->
I declare to you that <br/>
**the Lord** will build a house for you:

I will raise up your **offspring** to succeed you, <br/>
one of your own **sons**, <br/>
and I will establish **his kingdom**.

[1 Chronicles 17:10-11 (NIV) (p.3/3)](# "ref")

>>>
+ v12: He is the one who will build a house for me,
  and I will establish his throne forever.
+ not only **Solomon**, but royal line
  + to **Christ** the Son of David
+ **God** is the one with the **plan** / project
  + far beyond our small life **goals**
+ our responsibility is simply to be
  + good + faithful **servants** (Mt25:21)
  + not **boss** / owner!
+ v1(a) is about **success**, achievement
  + v1(b) is about **security**:

---
<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-eRQPWFijcx4-mtn_lake_boat.jpg") %> -->

+ Jesus gives us **comfort** in famous
  + Sermon on the **Mount**:

‘What shall we **eat**?’ <br/>

+ Worry takes our eyes off of
  + what should be our **priority**:

---
<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-8CwoHpZe3qE-hands.jpg") %> -->
But seek first **his kingdom** <br/>
and **his righteousness**,

and all these things <br>
will be given to you as well.

[Matthew 6:33 (NIV) (p.2/2)](# "ref")

>>>
+ honour God **first**

**toiling** for food to eat

[Psalm 127:2a (NIV)](# "ref")

>>>
+ rise for sake of being **early**:
  + stress, long to-do list

  + **body** tells me it's time to sleep
  + but I **refuse**: too much work to do
+ both: see sleep as **obstacle**
+ &rArr; Going to sleep is an act of **faith**
  + trust God will **provide**
  + **yield** to God's design for my body
+ "toiling": eat bread of **suffering**
+ Gen3: **work** was originally **good**
  + fruitful, **fulfilling**, fun
+ but because of mankind's **sin**:
  + work was **cursed**:

<!-- .slide: <%= bg("unsplash-8EFA86nI6yg-desert_cracks.jpg") %> -->

[Genesis 3:17 (NIV)](# "ref")

+ Particularly applies to Adam / **men**

  + kids **squander** it?

Children are a **heritage** from the Lord, <br>
offspring a **reward** from him.

[Psalm 127:3 (NIV)](# "ref")

+ "heritage": **inheritance**
  + Heb: **possession**, not succession
  + inheritance is **Father's** property
+ we the **heirs** receive the inheritance
  + only bc of **relation** w/the Father
  + we ourselves are **adopted** as heirs
+ children are owned by **God**
+ entrusted to our **stewardship**

<!-- .slide: <%= bg("pixabay-1557473-arrow.jpg") %> -->
Like **arrows** in the hands of a **warrior** <br/>
are children born in one’s youth.

**Blessed** is the man <br/>
whose quiver is full of them.

[Psalm 127:4-5 (NIV)](# "ref")

>>>
+ as with sleep: **grace**, not earned
  + God does not **owe** us children
  + not a **right**, but a **gift**
+ "warrior": mighty **champion** of valour
  + "arrows": directed **intent**
+ opportunity to **shape** their character
  + by **words** and my **actions**
+ then **release** to fly by God's grace
+ **quiver** full: gift, not a **pursuit**
  + not just cranking out more **babies**
  + every child a **blessing**
  + responsibility to **train** each one
